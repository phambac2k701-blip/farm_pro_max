# CHARACTERS

## AN — Protagonist
- Age: 20
- Role: university freshman; former Minh Khai student
- Current life: lives with a relative during break; studying away from hometown
- Personality: observant, pragmatic, socially cautious
- Desire: find Khang and leave the school behind
- Fear: being responsible for another person's disappearance by ignoring them
- Shame: dismissed Khang's earlier archive concerns
- Relationship to Khang: close friend, occasional skeptic
- Relationship to Ninth: initially treats it as a missing-person question
- Knows: school layout, some former students, current social habits
- Thinks he knows: old school records are boring but reliable
- Wrong about: reliability is not the same as truth
- Hides: he deleted one of Khang's earlier messages because it annoyed him
- Narrative function: player viewpoint and eventual bearer-risk
- Gameplay evidence: phone, inspection, comparison, evidence journal
- Foreshadowing: avoids rooms whose layouts he remembers incorrectly
- Payoff: realizes memory is active interpretation rather than passive storage

## KHANG — Missing friend
- Age: 20
- Role: part-time digitization assistant for school alumni archive
- Current life: obsessive, underemployed, socially independent
- Personality: patient with evidence, impatient with people
- Desire: prove that 09 refers to a real person
- Fear: discovering that everyone knew and accepted the omission
- Shame: he wants to be the first person to “solve” the school’s secret
- Relationship to An: trusts An enough to leave the message
- Relationship to Ninth: treats Ninth as an identity before understanding it as a role
- Knows: 09 appears across generations
- Thinks he knows: one person was erased
- Wrong about: the Ninth is singular
- Hides: he accessed restricted archive folders without permission
- Why: he feared the records would be cleaned before he finished
- Past decision: broke into the old PA room instead of reporting the inconsistency
- Present consequence: becomes the current Ninth-state
- Narrative function: inciting engine, false hypothesis, living consequence
- Gameplay evidence: phone messages, voice notes, handwritten cross-reference
- Foreshadowing: his messages shift pronouns from “người đó” to “nó”
- Payoff: current Ninth-state is Khang

## VÂN — Human antagonist / records coordinator
- Age: 44
- Role: records coordinator; former school-radio advisor
- Current life: responsible for anniversary archive cleanup
- Personality: controlled, practical, protective
- Desire: prevent incomplete evidence from becoming a public accusation
- Fear: a distorted archive harming former staff or students
- Shame: once participated in the decision to stop the 00:17 broadcast
- Relationship to An: initially patronizing; later candid
- Relationship to Khang: sees him as reckless
- Relationship to Ninth: knows the role existed but not its KCR implications
- Knows: the 2012 event, archive conventions, 09 template
- Thinks she knows: the matter was contained
- Wrong about: removing ambiguity prevents harm
- Hides: she destroyed one duplicated paper list years ago
- Why: she believed the list would cause rumor without context
- Past decision: approved the administrative cleanup
- Present consequence: becomes a reluctant witness
- Narrative function: human resistance with legitimate concerns
- Evidence: archive inventory, authorization logs, repair requests
- Foreshadow: corrects An's wording from “người” to “vị trí” once
- Payoff: admits the role was real and used

## PHƯƠNG — 29 in present day
- Role: former student from the 2012 event; now school alumni volunteer
- Desire: avoid reopening an embarrassing night
- Fear: being remembered only for the accident
- Connection: the injured student from 2012
- Secret: remembers Minh, the informal Ninth occupant that night
- Narrative function: humanizes the past and prevents the mystery from becoming pure puzzle

## MINH — historical Ninth occupant
- Role: student, unofficial after-hours operator in 2012
- Desire: make the 00:17 incident count as real
- Fear: being blamed for a situation he did not cause
- Secret: copied the raw audio and hid it in a notebook
- Narrative function: first concrete Ninth identity; bridge from role to person

## MY — current school radio student
- Age: 17
- Desire: protect the radio club and keep it running
- Fear: being used as another scapegoat
- Function: contemporary counterpoint; shows the role still exists in modified form

## LỘC — retired security guard
- Age: 63
- Desire: keep promises to former students
- Fear: being accused of letting students stay overnight
- Function: physical access, route memory, human memory that conflicts usefully with documents

## HẠNH — records clerk
- Age: 38
- Desire: finish digitization correctly and keep procedures consistent
- Fear: making an administrative error
- Function: demonstrates how institutional omission can continue without malicious intent

## Character agency rule
No supporting character exists solely to tell An a clue. Every character has a current goal that can conflict with An's investigation.
