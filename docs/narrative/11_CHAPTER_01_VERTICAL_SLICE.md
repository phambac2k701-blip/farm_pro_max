# CHAPTER 1 — TIN NHẮN 00:17

## Purpose
Hook the player, establish Khang, introduce school-night exploration, create the first contradiction, and demonstrate the first KCR without explaining it.

## Target duration
25–35 minutes for first playthrough.

## Starting knowledge
- Khang has gone missing.
- Khang was digitizing school records.
- An has received “Tao tìm thấy người thứ chín.”
- An expects to find a person or a document.

## Scene 1.1 — Opening image
Time: 00:24.
Location: school gate.
Player control begins immediately after a 2-second still image of rain hitting the metal gate.
First sound: distant traffic, rain, fluorescent buzz from guard shelter.
Phone vibration cuts the ambience.

Player sees: locked main gate half-open; dark administration block; one lit corridor window.
No supernatural effect.

Interaction: phone.
Evidence C01.

Message transcript:
Khang — 00:17
“Tao tìm thấy người thứ chín.”

An reply draft is not selectable; the player can only continue.
Second message:
“Trường cũ. Phòng phát thanh.”
Third message, sent 12 seconds later:
“Đừng gọi ai.”

Phone battery: 18%.

Exit condition: player walks to guard shelter.

## Scene 1.2 — Guard shelter
Location: gate shelter.
NPC: Lộc is absent; only a desk, key rack, notebook.
Player goal: find a way into the old wing.

Interactables:
- visitor notebook
- key rack
- flashlight
- plastic raincoat

Environmental clue: keys 1–8 are labeled; one old hook has label 09 but no key.
Evidence C05 seed.

Interaction beat: An tries main key, fails. A side entrance opens because the latch is already loose.
No cutscene.

## Scene 1.3 — Corridor tutorial
Location: first corridor.
Teach: focus inspection, door opening, drawer pull, physical object pickup.

Environmental story:
- class photographs on wall
- old timetable
- rainwater under window
- one trophy cabinet

Optional clue: C14, timetable still lists 00:17 as “night equipment check” in tiny handwriting.

Unease beat: one classroom door is open even though An remembers it always being closed. No sound cue.

## Scene 1.4 — Empty classroom
Location: former classroom.
Player goal: find a document or route toward PA room.

Interactables:
- teacher desk
- drawer
- class photo
- desk arrangement

Mandatory evidence: C03, an old eight-name activity roster tucked behind a textbook.

Document text:
“Đội Phát thanh — phân công chính thức: 8 học sinh. Các vị trí khác do phụ trách quyết định theo tình hình.”

Surface reading: normal administrative wording.
True reading: “other positions” is the first surviving admission that the eight-name list was incomplete.

Player action: compare roster with classroom photo.
Observation: photo composition leaves a cropped margin at right.
Evidence C02.

## Scene 1.5 — First contradiction
Location: same classroom.
Player reopens drawer after reading roster.
The drawer contains a small adhesive label underneath newer paper: `09`.

No music.
The fluorescent light buzz drops in pitch for one second.

This is the first clear contradiction: there is a physical 09 marker where the official roster has none.

KCR trigger preparation: C03 + C05.

## Scene 1.6 — Return to corridor
Player returns to corridor.
A distant school bell sounds once, but no clock changes.
The player can inspect the hallway photo board.

Optional clue: C14.

KCR-A occurs only after the player has seen both C03 and C07. C07 is reached by following the PA-room sign, not yet entering the room.

## Scene 1.7 — Radio room threshold
Location: corridor outside PA room.
Door label: “PHÒNG PHÁT THANH”.
Under it, scratched paint leaves the faint outline of `09`.

Player opens door.
Inside: eight chairs/headsets visible.
A ninth headphone cable lies behind a cabinet but cannot be reached yet.

Audio: no horror cue, only rain and amplifier hum.

Mandatory evidence C07: cassette/index card marked `09 / 00:17`.

Player can read:
“KHÔNG PHÁT — lưu nội bộ.”

This raises main question: what happened at 00:17?

## Scene 1.8 — First KCR
Trigger: C03 + C07 collected.
Player leaves radio room, then returns.

Before:
8 chairs, 8 headsets, one empty wall hook.

After:
9 chairs. Ninth chair faces slightly away from the desk. A headset rests on it, plugged into nothing.

No visual transition.
No text.
No scare sting.

Audio: when the player gets within 1 metre, a faint room-tone recording plays through the unplugged headset for 2.5 seconds: breathing + chair scrape.

Player cannot take the headset yet.

Narrative meaning: the role is beginning to become physically resolvable.

## Scene 1.9 — Chapter climax
Player inspects headset.
Phone vibrates.
New message from Khang:
“Đừng để nó thành người.”

Player looks toward the eighth desk.
Nothing happens.

Then the PA speaker clicks.
Khang's voice, distant and compressed:
“An? Mày tới rồi à?”

The line is only 6 words. No exposition.

Exit condition: player opens corridor door.

## Final image
The corridor wall photo is unchanged at first glance.
On closer inspection, the reflection on the glass shows one extra shoulder outside the player’s field of view. It vanishes when An looks directly at it.

## Final audio
Rain continues. Under it, a barely audible relay click at 00:17:00.

## Cliffhanger
The player has heard Khang inside a school system that should not have a live connection.

## Chapter hypothesis state
Default: “There is an omitted person.”
Advanced players may form: “09 is a recurring role.”
The game never confirms either.
