# MYSTERY ARCHITECTURE

## Core question ladder
1. Why did Khang disappear?
2. What does 09 represent?
3. Why do sources disagree about the Ninth?
4. Did one person get erased?
5. Why does the physical space change after evidence is found?
6. Why is Khang becoming more present in the archive while less present in the normal world?
7. What happened at 00:17 in 2012?
8. What is the Ninth: person, role, or mechanism?
9. What happens when the player becomes the strongest witness?

## False theory progression
### Theory 1: One erased student
Supported by cropped photos and missing names.
Broken by contradictory names in the same role.

### Theory 2: Supernatural duplicate
Supported by changing room states and audio.
Broken by the mundane continuity of the 09 role across documents.

### Theory 3: Institutional cover-up only
Supported by Vân's actions.
Broken by physical KCR events that cannot be reduced to simple record tampering.

### Theory 4: The Ninth is a role with a reality effect
Strongest pre-finale model.

## Reveal chain
SETUP: eight official students.
→ ASSUMPTION: one ninth student was erased.
→ REVEAL: multiple people are documented as 09.
→ RECONTEXTUALIZATION: the “contradictory” photos are each locally correct.
→ CONSEQUENCE: the game world gains a ninth physical state.

SETUP: Khang disappears while researching.
→ ASSUMPTION: he was threatened.
→ REVEAL: he has become the current bearer of 09.
→ RECONTEXTUALIZATION: his messages are not evidence from outside the archive; they are transmissions from inside the role-state.
→ CONSEQUENCE: An's own knowledge becomes dangerous because he may inherit the slot.

## What the mystery must never reveal too early
- the 2012 incident's exact injury mechanism;
- Minh's name;
- the fact that 09 is a role;
- Khang's current state;
- the operational KCR threshold.

## What player can infer early
- there are eight official names;
- there is a recurring ninth position;
- the evidence conflicts in a patterned way;
- 00:17 recurs.

## Core answer
The Ninth is a repeated institutional role for uncredited after-hours responsibility. The role became entangled with memory, records, devices and place strongly enough that when a witness reconstructs it, linked representations begin to complete the omitted state.

## Ontological ambiguity boundary
The game never needs to prove whether the phenomenon is literally supernatural. It must prove that the phenomenon is real enough to alter player strategy and that its effects follow consistent rules.


## Production state boundary
C35 in Chapter 6 establishes `khang_signal_found` only. It does not open the final archive route. The final route requires the player to possess both historical-event knowledge and current-bearer knowledge and to explicitly test the hidden route in Chapter 9. This preserves the intended reveal order without changing canon.
