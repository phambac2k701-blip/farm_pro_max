The after-hours duty continues informally. Different students perform it. The obsolete “09” slot remains in reused templates, old folders, and occasional notes.

At least three later students become “the Ninth” in local memory because they occupied the same practical role.

Khang notices 09 appears under incompatible names. He creates a private cross-reference file.

He accesses the old PA room and archive route. His messages become unstable. He disappears from normal contact.
