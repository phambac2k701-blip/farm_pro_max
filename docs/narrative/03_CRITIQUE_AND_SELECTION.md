# CRITIQUE / SELECTION

## Direction A critique
1. **Coincidence risk:** Too many independent records could not accidentally use the same placeholder. Repair: the placeholder originates from a real administrative shortcut and is reused through a known archive template.
2. **Cliché risk:** “school hides scandal” is common. Repair: the hidden thing is responsibility architecture, not a single crime.
3. **Clue fairness:** Player could conclude “ghost student” too early. Repair: early clues must point to multiple possible identities and one recurring identifier.
4. **KCR risk:** Reality changes could feel magical. Repair: every KCR is triggered by a cross-source evidence threshold represented by explicit GameState.
5. **Character risk:** Khang could exist only to disappear. Repair: his investigation has a thesis he is wrong about: he thinks there is one erased student.

## Direction B critique
- Predictable ghost-witness solution.
- Less useful for different source identities.
- Strong atmosphere, weaker institutional theme.

## Direction C critique
- Excellent theme of invisible labor.
- More likely to become a social drama than horror investigation.
- Harder to justify the room and object changes without abstraction.

## Direction D critique
- Best environmental horror.
- Weakest missing-friend causal engine.
- Risks puzzle without emotional center.

## Direction E critique
- Good long-form structure.
- Strong setup for repeated motifs.
- Too folklore-like and risks a hereditary curse feeling.

## Selection rationale
Adopt A as the causal spine. Borrow C’s idea that labor and responsibility can be split from attribution, and E’s recurrence across school generations. Reject any implication that the Ninth is genetically inherited, cursed bloodline, or simple ghost identity.

## Acceptance criteria for Canon v1
- At least three distinct people can truthfully appear as the Ninth in different artifacts.
- There is one specific past event that explains why the role exists.
- The role has a mundane institutional reason to exist.
- KCR has deterministic triggers and visible consequences.
- A player can solve the core mystery without knowing the final metaphysical interpretation.
- No essential clue is available only once.
