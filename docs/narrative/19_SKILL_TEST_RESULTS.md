# PROJECT-LOCAL SKILL TEST RESULTS

## Test fixture
Three synthetic candidates, intentionally not part of Người Thứ Chín canon.

### Synthetic A — Erased student
A student was physically removed from all school records after a scandal; the ghost appears in every old photo.

### Synthetic B — Institutional role
A school repeatedly uses a nameless ninth duty position. Different students occupy it, and records collapse the role into one identity.

### Synthetic C — Reality-writing archive
A digital archive changes the past whenever enough users agree on a record.

## Expected distinctions
Causal mechanism, thematic argument, player actions, evidence architecture, KCR behavior.

## Critique results
A:
- cliché risk: high, because “erased student ghost” is a familiar horror pattern;
- gameplay risk: low inference space after first reveal;
- clue risk: contradiction becomes cosmetic unless the ghost identity matters.

B:
- causal mechanism distinct: role/person conflation;
- gameplay: strong comparison of records, photos, spatial states;
- theme: responsibility and institutional memory;
- KCR can be deterministic without needing universal reality rewrite.

C:
- powerful speculative mechanic;
- causality becomes too broad because arbitrary “enough users” is difficult to stage fairly;
- risk of turning every document into a reality control button;
- could overwhelm the intimate school setting.

## Test verdict
The skill distinguished all three by mechanism/theme/gameplay/KCR and identified different failure modes. Candidate B showed the best compatibility with the project constraints.

## Generic-output test
The critique was rejected whenever it could apply unchanged to all three candidates. Required findings to reference candidate-specific mechanics, clue dependencies, or player actions.
