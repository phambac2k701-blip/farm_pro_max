# ENDINGS

## Ending states
No morality meter. Outcomes use concrete knowledge and preservation states.

### Ending A — “Đủ hồ sơ”
Requirements:
- core historical evidence found;
- 2012 event understood;
- Khang located;
- player preserves the historical/contextual archive but deliberately does **not** stabilize the live current-witness linkage as an official record.

Result:
An restores the missing historical context and records 09 as an unassigned recurring duty role. Khang returns to ordinary space because the present bearer-link is allowed to collapse instead of being reassigned. The cost is evidentiary: Khang's current Ninth-state can no longer be fully proven. Live-signal attribution becomes source-less/unstable, and some current-state photographs no longer retain Khang consistently. The 2012 history remains preserved.

Theme payoff: historical truth can be preserved without naming a new bearer, but the present phenomenon becomes partly unverifiable.

### Ending B — “Người ghi tên”
Requirements:
- high evidence completeness;
- An understands KCR;
- player chooses to preserve the **complete** historical + present-day witness chain;
- Action B is selected, setting `witness_burden = true` and preserving the current-witness linkage.

Result:
The archive records `09 — current witness: An`. Khang returns, and the current-state evidence remains coherent and verifiable because An has accepted the witness designation. An disappears from one old photo in a way no one questions. The final chair remains. The exact metaphysics stay unresolved.

Theme payoff: preserving the complete record, including the present phenomenon, requires a living witness to carry its burden.

### Ending C — “Không ai là người thứ chín”
Requirements:
- player destroys or withholds the final evidence needed to stabilize the current role;
- Action C is selected;
- Khang escapes through the unresolved route;
- key understanding remains incomplete.

Result:
The school returns to eight seats. Khang survives. One anonymous audio file remains outside the official archive. The player cannot prove the full mechanism.

Theme payoff: removing the record removes the phenomenon, but does not erase the past.

### Ending D — “Bản ghi sạch”
Requirements:
- player sides operationally with Vân;
- incomplete evidence set;
- destroys critical duplicate evidence.

Result:
Public history remains clean. Months later, a new student finds a timetable with a 00:17 duty slot. No explicit KCR occurs during the ending, but the Ninth position persists as an institutional possibility.

Theme payoff: the clean record outlives the truth it excluded.

## Ending fairness
Every ending is foreshadowed. None depends on hidden morality arithmetic. Ending differences come from evidence, understanding, preservation, witness designation, and specific choices. KCR-H occurs only on Ending B because only that ending explicitly assigns the present witness role.
