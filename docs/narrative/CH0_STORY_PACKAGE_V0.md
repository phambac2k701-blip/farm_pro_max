# 1. Chapter function

Chapter 0 is the opening entry into Hanoi/UET student life.

Current user direction:
- begin small and ordinary rather than dramatic;
- let the player learn Hanoi/student-life behavior by doing;
- use mundane mistakes as comedy;
- keep humor participatory rather than narrator-explained;
- allow locally wrong choices that create funny content and later reconverge;
- end the chapter after the first UET visit and the return trip toward the rented room.

No final-game ending logic belongs here.

---

# 2. Tone contract

Required:
- grounded student-life reality;
- first-person playable presentation;
- concise dialogue;
- deadpan / absurd / "vô tri" humor;
- contemporary/trend-aware rhythm when appropriate;
- small reversals/twists;
- option-specific reactions;
- no arbitrary bad endings for ordinary mistakes;
- believable in-world reconvergence;
- no long narrator monologues explaining jokes.

A joke should ideally still work if most narration is removed.

## CH0-S02 — First attempt to catch a bus

Source type:
- core lived material from user.

User-recalled basis:
- protagonist is not standing at a marked bus stop;
- protagonist waves at approaching buses expecting them to stop;
- buses do not stop;
- this happens repeatedly enough to become funny/confusing;
- eventually a bus staff member/conductor calls out that the protagonist has to go to the proper stop.

Presentation principle:
- let the player physically wave / attempt to flag the bus;
- show the buses continuing past;
- do not immediately explain the mistake;
- repetition may increase the joke;
- staff correction is the reveal.

Possible small twist:
The protagonist assumes buses can be hailed anywhere; the world reveals the rule by refusing to cooperate.

No failure screen.

Exit:
player moves to the actual bus stop.

## CH0-S06 — First arrival at UET / finding the correct room

Source type:
- coordinator proposal accepted by user as current direction.

Player arrives and has a short first-look beat.

The immediate practical problem:
**where exactly am I supposed to go?**

Gameplay should shift from bus timing to observation/navigation/social choice.

### Option A — Ask a guard/staff member

Direction given is technically correct but only partially useful, e.g.:
- go straight;
- turn;
- go upstairs;
- ask again.

Comedy comes from receiving correct information that still leaves the player slightly lost.

### Option B — Follow a confident-looking student group

The group appears to know where it is going.

Player follows.

Later reveal:
- they may also be lost;
- or they were going somewhere unrelated;
- or their confidence was unjustified.

Suggested comic structure:
someone notices the protagonist following and asks, in effect, whether the protagonist was following them.

Short awkward pause.

The group/player must correct course.

### Option C — Trust signs / phone and navigate independently

Player gets close to the correct place but opens/approaches the wrong room.

Possible visual punchline:
- room full of people looks toward the player;
- player quietly closes the door;
- only then notices the correct sign/direction nearby.

No narrator explanation required.

### Reconvergence

All branches naturally lead to the same correct administrative/target area.

The branch differences are local comedy and player expression, not permanent routes.

2. acknowledge the player's choice;
3. avoid arbitrary bad endings;
4. have a believable in-world correction;
5. reconverge when no persistent consequence is justified.

Chapter 0 currently does not require a major persistent branch.
