# CORE PILLARS

## Non-negotiable identity
- First-person 3D investigation/horror.
- Non-combat.
- Exploration and tactile interaction.
- Environmental storytelling.
- Evidence-driven authored mystery.
- Knowledge Changes Reality.
- Vietnam must affect causality, not merely aesthetics.
- Horror comes from wrong counts, absence, repetition, social memory, records, audio, and familiar places behaving incorrectly.

## Story pillars
1. A missing friend starts the investigation.
2. The Ninth is initially underdetermined.
3. Contradictory evidence is intentional and explainable.
4. Player knowledge changes future observations and world state.
5. A replay should make early clues more meaningful.
6. The core timeline is reconstructable.
7. Ambiguity is allowed in interpretation, not in basic causality.
8. The protagonist causes discoveries and major turns.
9. Characters retain goals independent of the player.
10. The ending must pay off memory, record, responsibility, and erasure.

## Central theme
When institutions preserve a clean record by excluding a person or responsibility, the omission itself becomes a social act.

## Player fantasy
You are not a ghost hunter. You are a person who becomes increasingly unwilling to accept a convenient version of the past.

## Player verbs
Observe, inspect, compare, retrieve, replay, trace, return, cross-check, listen, photograph, read, annotate, connect, preserve, confront.

## Production ceiling
The story is designed around modular school locations, a small core cast, physical props, audio evidence, and state-driven variants. No bespoke combat, enemy AI, or large open-world dependency.
