## 4. Global audit
### Plot logic
PASS. Major outcomes follow from prior character choices, evidence, and state. No core coincidence required.

### Character agency
PASS. An, Khang, Vân, Phương, Lộc and Hạnh each have goals that exist independently of clue delivery. NPCs may resist or withhold.

### Mystery fairness
PASS. Major conclusions have redundant evidence paths. The player can predict the role before the game confirms the full mechanism.

### Clue fairness
PASS. Mandatory clues are recoverable. Optional clues improve confidence rather than gating unique truth.

### Timeline
PASS. 2012 event, later role recurrence, 2026 digitization, Khang investigation, disappearance, and current investigation remain causally ordered.

### Location logic
PASS. School spaces connect in a coherent route; external locations occur only after evidence sends the player there.

### Document logic
PASS. Archive contradictions are caused by reused labels, differing provenance layers, and deliberate administrative smoothing, not magical paperwork.

### Social logic
PASS. Vân's behavior is institutional and protective rather than cartoonishly villainous. Phương and Lộc do not become exposition machines.

### KCR
PASS after trigger correction. All 8 KCR events are deterministic, state-driven, uniquely manifested, and have documented before/after states.

### Gameplay integration
PASS. Each chapter uses investigation verbs that create knowledge. No chapter relies only on travel + text dump.

### Pacing
PASS with production watchpoints:
- Ch5 must keep the route return motivated by C15.
- Ch6 must keep rental-room search shorter than school archive sections.
- Ch7 confrontation must remain evidence-backed rather than dialogue-heavy.

### Repetition
PASS. KCR manifestations vary: headset, stair route, badge slot, recorder state, archive folder, hidden door, reflection, witness line.

### Horror
PASS. The horror vocabulary remains absence, wrong count, spatial mismatch, impossible audio and social memory. No combat/gore requirement.

### Ending fairness
PASS after A/B tradeoff repair. Four endings derive from evidence completeness, what layer of evidence is preserved, witness designation and procedural choice. Ending A sacrifices stable proof of the present witness-state; Ending B preserves that proof by assigning witness burden. No morality meter.

### Theme
PASS. Theme is dramatized through what characters record, omit, name and refuse to name.
