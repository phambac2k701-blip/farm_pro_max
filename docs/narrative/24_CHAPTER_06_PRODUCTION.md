Production-ready narrative specification.

## 0. Chapter contract
- Time: 03:00–03:55.
- Primary locations: Khang's rental room, corridor outside room, stair/utility access used by the rental building, transition back toward school.
- Starting state: Ch5 complete; `knowledge_multiple_occupants = true`; KCR-B active; Khang's disappearance is believed connected to the Ninth-state.
- Primary question: Khang đã gặp chuyện gì và hiện đang ở đâu?
- Player verbs: search, reconstruct, inspect, replay, compare, call, follow signal.
- Narrative answer: Khang is alive but has become the current bearer/witness-anchor of the Ninth-state.

## 5. Scene C6.4 — Recorder E18
Entry:
- C22 linked to C26.

Objective:
- Hear Khang's last deliberate warning.

Player action:
- Pick up digital recorder.
- Press physical play button.
- Move closer as volume drops.

Mandatory evidence:
- C27 / rental-room recorder.

Pre-recorded transcript:
Khang: `Nếu mày nghe được cái này thì đừng cố gọi tao bằng tên.`
The recording ends with a physical stop/click.

After playback:
An, live in the room: `Khang?`
There is no immediate verbal answer.

Design note:
- An's spoken `Khang?` is an authored live line. It is not a player dialogue branch and is not part of the recording.
- The pre-recorded file never answers a future live utterance.
- The causal payoff for naming Khang is deferred to the post-KCR-D `listen live` signal in C6.6.

Audio:
- Recorder playback is intentionally dry and close.
- After An says the name, the room remains silent except for a slight second room-tone layer; no voice replies yet.

Lighting:
- Desk lamp remains stable.
- No screen effect.

Narrative meaning:
- The recording warns against stabilizing Khang by name; the consequence is tested later through a genuinely live KCR-linked signal.

Exit condition:
- Player finishes the full recording.
